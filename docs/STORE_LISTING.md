**App name:** Skyloop: Sky City Tycoon

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Roll, build and outsmart rivals in a 4-player board game on a floating island.

**Full description:**

Build the richest city in the sky.

Skyloop is a fast, modern board game for 4 players. Roll the dice, choose where to land, buy
streets, raise them into landmarks and collect rent from your rivals. A match takes about 10 minutes.

**Every roll is a choice**
Move by the total, or spend a steer token to move by one die. Plan routes onto your own streets
and away from your rivals' rent.

**Sealed auctions and hostile takeovers**
Pass on a tile and everyone bids in secret. Buy out a rival's street, buildings included.

**Four tycoons, four powers**
The Developer builds overnight, the Shark squeezes double rent, the Broker swaps streets, and the
Drifter is always on the move.

**Climb the tables**
Start in your home city and move up to the high-roller tables. Each table has its own twist.

**Something new every day**
Daily rewards, a daily spin, fresh missions and a weekly trophy race.

**Make it yours**
Golden pawns, neon dice and 12 avatars.

Skyloop is free to play. No pay-to-win: purchases are cosmetic.
