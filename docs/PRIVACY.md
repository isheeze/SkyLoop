Skyloop ("the game") is published by [PUBLISHER NAME]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect
- **A random player ID.** Created when you first play and stored on your device. It isn't linked to
  your name, phone number, email or advertising ID.
- **Your display name and avatar choice.** These are what you type or pick in the game. Other players
  see them in matches and leaderboards. Don't use your real full name if you'd rather not.
- **Your country.** Worked out on your device from its language and time-zone settings, and used only
  to show local city names and the country leaderboard. We don't use GPS or location permissions.
- **Game progress.** Coins, gems, level, trophies, items, match results and mission progress.
- **Quick-chat messages.** These are preset phrases only; there is no free-text chat.

## What we don't collect
We don't collect contacts, photos, precise location, phone numbers, email addresses or device
identifiers. The current version has no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

## How it's used
Only to run the game: matchmaking, saving progress, leaderboards and rewards.

## Where it's stored
On our game server ([HOSTING PROVIDER, REGION]), and in the game's own storage on your device.
Network traffic is encrypted (TLS).

## Sharing
We don't sell or share your data. Other players can see your display name, avatar, level and
leaderboard score.

## Keeping and deleting data
Progress is kept while you play. To delete your data, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the player name
shown on your Profile page and we'll remove it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the
data on your device.

## Children
Skyloop isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

## Changes
We'll update this page when the game changes what it collects, and change the date above.
